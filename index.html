ℹ️ About MK PEDIA

MK PEDIA is a multilingual digital learning library for students, teachers and lifelong learners in Sri Lanka.

📤 How to contribute

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Upload a PDF (maximum 20 MB).
  3. Enter title, grade, subject and medium.
  4. Submit for review; it appears after approval.

✅ Community guidelines

  • Share only material you have permission to share.
  • Do not share private records or copyrighted content without permission.
  • Use accurate metadata and be respectful.

🔐 Privacy

Saved items and history stay in this browser. Avoid sensitive information in public areas.
