ℹ️ About MK PEDIA

MK PEDIA is a multilingual digital learning library for students, teachers and lifelong learners in Sri Lanka.

📤 How to contribute

  1. Upload your learning material to Google Drive.
  2. Set access to “Anyone with the link – Viewer”.
  3. Open Add Link and enter the correct title, grade, subject and medium.
  4. Submit it for review. It appears in the public library after approval.

✅ Community guidelines

  • Share only educational material that you have permission to share.
  • Do not share private records or copyrighted books without permission.
  • Use accurate titles, subjects, grades and language labels.
  • Be respectful in community chat. Never post passwords or private details.
  • Report broken or misleading links through the Requests page.

🔐 Privacy note

Saved items and recent history stay in this browser. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public areas.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Why is my submitted resource not visible yet?

New submissions wait for administrator approval.

Why does a Drive file ask me for access?

The owner may not have enabled “Anyone with the link – Viewer”.

Can I upload a file directly here?

The current version stores a Google Drive sharing link. It does not upload the file itself.

Are saved resources available on another phone?

Not yet. Saved items are stored locally on this device and browser.
