Are MKLIO tools free?

The tools in this release are available without registration or subscription. Some external services may have their own limits.

Are my files uploaded?

Many PDF, image and text tools process files locally in your browser. Background removal uses Cloudinary and uploads the selected image to the configured Cloudinary account.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to adapt to modern mobile browsers, tablets and desktop screens.
