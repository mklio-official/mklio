Privacy Policy

This page describes the information processed by MKLIO CINEMA.

Information stored

  • Public movie entries may include title, actor name, image URLs, description, Google Drive link, likes and creation date.
  • Comments submitted by visitors are stored in Firebase Firestore and displayed publicly.
  • Administrator sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. The administrator’s password is not stored in this website’s source code.

Third-party services

MKLIO CINEMA uses GitHub Pages to host the website, Firebase Authentication and Firestore for administration and catalogue data, and Google Drive’s embedded player for playback. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
