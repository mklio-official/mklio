Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Browser processing

Where stated, PDF conversion, image compression/resizing, age calculation, QR generation, password generation and word counting are designed to run in your browser. These tools do not intentionally send the file or text you enter to MKLIO servers.

Third-party processing

The Background Remover feature uploads the image you select to your configured Cloudinary account using an unsigned upload preset. Cloudinary processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not upload confidential or sensitive images unless you understand and accept that service's terms.

The Currency Converter contacts a public exchange-rate provider to retrieve rates for the selected base currency. The request includes the currency code, not the amount you enter.

PDF and QR libraries may be loaded from public content delivery networks. Your browser connects to those providers to download the library code.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This starter package does not include analytics, advertising or account sign-in code. If these are added later, this policy must be updated to explain the services and data involved before publication.

Data retention

MKLIO does not provide file storage or user accounts in this starter release. Browser downloads and temporary browser memory are managed by your device and browser.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page. Set a real monitored contact email before publishing.
