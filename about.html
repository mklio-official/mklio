About MKLIO

MKLIO is a growing collection of simple online utilities for everyday digital tasks. The first release focuses on PDF and image conversion, calculators, QR codes, text counting and other practical helpers.

Our goal is to make common tasks easier through clear interfaces, accessible design and privacy-conscious processing. Where practical, tools work directly in your browser. Some features rely on clearly identified third-party services.

MKLIO is an independent project. It is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Cloudinary or other third-party service providers.
